# [RB-021] Implement room/soft/strong calibration

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M1 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Audio

## Outcome

Make comfortable relative sound levels usable across devices without escalating effort.

## Implementation

1. Collect valid window percentiles/medians for three explicit stages.
2. Apply range/noise/clipping checks and keep previous calibration on failure.
3. Store only versioned numeric thresholds; invalidate on route changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Too-noisy or too-small range shows recovery/Touch, not forced louder prompts.
- [ ] Soft and strong can be achieved comfortably on test devices.
- [ ] No hardware identifier or audio recording is stored.

## Required evidence

Calibration fixtures and device observation sheet.
