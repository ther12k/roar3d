# [RB-010] Implement ball impulse and rolling resistance

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M1 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Gameplay

## Outcome

Build a stable spherical physics controller rather than visually animating a fake trajectory.

## Implementation

1. Apply each accepted impulse exactly once on the physics tick.
2. Implement support-aware rolling resistance without suppressing flight/gravity.
3. Keep cosmetic transforms separate and expose settlement metrics.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Physics rate stays fixed at 60 Hz across quality tiers.
- [ ] Flat roll distance is measured at four powers.
- [ ] No per-frame impulse multiplication or nonuniform collider scaling.

## Required evidence

Regression scene recordings and tuning table.
