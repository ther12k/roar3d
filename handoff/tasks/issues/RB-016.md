# [RB-016] Implement cup capture and completion ownership

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M1 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Gameplay

## Outcome

Finish only when the ball actually qualifies for cup capture.

## Implementation

1. Build visual opening/rim and separate eligibility trigger.
2. Check distance, height, and speed; send one terminal completion.
3. Run cosmetic sink/celebration after scoring authority disables fall reactions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Slow valid entry completes once.
- [ ] High flyover, fast pass, and underside entry do not win.
- [ ] Animation signals cannot duplicate a result.

## Required evidence

Cup regression scene and tests.
