# [RB-004] Validate Jolt ball, ramp, and collision behavior

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M0 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Gameplay

## Outcome

Prove the chosen physics baseline can support rolling, ramps, and high-speed impacts.

## Implementation

1. Create diagnostic flat-roll, ramp, and thin-barrier scenes.
2. Use the proposed sphere scale, fixed physics rate, and CCD; sweep candidate impulses.
3. Record tuning concerns and engine-specific limitations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No unacceptable tunneling in the diagnostic power range.
- [ ] A repeatable resting/rolling approach is documented.
- [ ] Selected engine, tick rate, and tuning version are recorded.

## Required evidence

Diagnostic scenes and physical-device/desktop result notes.
