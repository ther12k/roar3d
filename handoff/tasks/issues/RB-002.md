# [RB-002] Select physical Android test matrix

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M0 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** S

**Owner role:** QA

## Outcome

Replace unsupported device assumptions with an explicit minimum/target test inventory.

## Implementation

1. Choose minimum-tier, mid-tier, and alternate-GPU phones from available inventory.
2. Record OS, renderer support, audio routes, screen aspect and safe-area characteristics.
3. Define the baseline performance/privacy evidence worksheet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least three actual devices are identified with owners/access.
- [ ] Proposed minimum Android support is marked unapproved until tests pass.
- [ ] Bluetooth, interruptions, and notch/navigation conditions are represented.

## Required evidence

Device matrix and planned test assignments.
