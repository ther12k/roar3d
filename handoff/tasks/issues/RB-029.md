# [RB-029] Establish mobile lighting, materials, and quality tiers

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M2 · **Priority:** P1 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Rendering/Art

## Outcome

Match reference mood through measured stylization rather than expensive cinematic effects.

## Implementation

1. Create shared turf/wood/stone materials and lightweight waterfall/background treatment.
2. Implement measured shadow/particle/resolution quality settings.
3. Keep gameplay cues visible in the lowest quality tier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No quality tier changes physics, collider, tick rate, or scoring.
- [ ] Representative scene meets selected target budgets or records a blocker.
- [ ] Low quality preserves cup/gate/portal clarity.

## Required evidence

Quality comparison captures and frame-time report.
