# [RB-017] Implement stars, attempt limits, and unlock rules

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M1 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** S

**Owner role:** Gameplay

## Outcome

Encode unambiguous scoring and sequential progression without an economy.

## Implementation

1. Implement 3/2/1-star thresholds and twelve-stroke attempt resolution.
2. Apply penalties before failure resolution and allow completion on stroke twelve.
3. Derive next-hole and cosmetic unlocks from completed levels.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Par/par+1/par+2 produce 3/2/1 stars for valid completion.
- [ ] Eleven strokes plus shot plus fall resolves thirteen and fails.
- [ ] One-star CC06 unlocks PP01 and Panda.
