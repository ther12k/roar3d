# [RB-024] Handle pause, background, route changes, and scene disposal

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M1 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Audio/Platform

## Outcome

Make capture and simulation lifecycle safe across real application interruptions.

## Implementation

1. Cancel uncommitted commands on pause/background and invalidate capture IDs.
2. Pause/resume rolling physics and gate phase consistently.
3. Stop input/listeners and dispose sessions on scene exit; do not auto-restart mic.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Privacy indicator and capture ownership tests pass on target devices.
- [ ] Returning during a prior hold cannot launch a shot.
- [ ] Thirty game entry/exit cycles leave one live session at most.

## Required evidence

Lifecycle matrix and stress-run logs.
