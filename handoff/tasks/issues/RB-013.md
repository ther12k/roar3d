# [RB-013] Implement Ready, Follow, and Overview cameras

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M1 · **Priority:** P1 · **Relative size:** M

**Owner role:** Gameplay

## Outcome

Maintain readable course framing through aiming, flight, reset, and device aspect changes.

## Implementation

1. Use authored course bounds and next-target framing.
2. Implement smoothed follow and fixed Ready-only Overview with paused simulation.
3. Lock camera during capture; add Reduced Motion handling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Ball and target remain clear of HUD in reference sizes.
- [ ] Overview cannot be used to exploit unpaused hazard timing.
- [ ] Falling camera does not dive into the void.

## Required evidence

Portrait/narrow/tablet captures and camera state tests.
