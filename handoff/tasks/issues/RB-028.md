# [RB-028] Create modular course kit and collision wrappers

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M2 · **Priority:** P1 · **Relative size:** L

**Owner role:** Art/Gameplay

## Outcome

Enable varied holes without individually sculpting every complete course.

## Implementation

1. Build straight/corner/green/ramp/rail/cliff modules on consistent scale/grid.
2. Attach simplified hand-owned collision and clear pivots.
3. Verify seams with the actual ball at high and low speed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Module seams do not trap or bounce the ball unexpectedly.
- [ ] Reimports preserve wrapper interactions.
- [ ] Budget and provenance inventory covers all modules.

## Required evidence

Kit scene, collision regression run, asset inventory.
