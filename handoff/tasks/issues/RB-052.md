# [RB-052] Profile and optimize sustained mobile performance

**Scope:** mvp · **Milestone:** M4 · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** L

**Owner role:** Rendering/QA

## Outcome

Meet measured target/low tiers without changing gameplay behavior.

## Implementation

1. Run fifteen-minute release-like soaks on the full device matrix.
2. Identify CPU/GPU/audio/UI/resource bottlenecks and tune budgets.
3. Compare low/high tiers for visual cues and physics parity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Selected tiers meet agreed p95/p99/memory/thermal gates or block release.
- [ ] Repeated levels do not show unbounded resource growth.
- [ ] Optimizations do not change collision, tick rate, power, or score.

## Required evidence

Profiler report, before/after data, signed-off device support.
